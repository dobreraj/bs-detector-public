Privacy Policy

BS Detector Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

BS Detector is a simple entertainment app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

BS Detector does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app may store basic app data locally on your device, such as your counter state or app preferences, to support normal app functionality.

How Information Is Used

Any data stored by the app is used only to provide the core app experience, such as saving your current count, app settings, and audio-related preferences on your device.

Data Sharing

BS Detector does not sell your personal information. The app does not share personal information with third parties, except if required by law or to comply with a valid legal request.

Analytics and Tracking

BS Detector is not designed to track you across apps or websites. If analytics or similar services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Children's Privacy

BS Detector is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains there until you remove the app or clear the app's data from your device.

Your Choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the GitHub profile or repository contact information at github.com/dobreraj.

Published Policy URL
